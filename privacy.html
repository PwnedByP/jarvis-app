Jarvis privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who uses it

Jarvis is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own home server. It has no other users and no sign-up.

What it accesses

When the owner connects their Google account, Jarvis can:

  • read, add and move events in Google Calendar;
  • read, add and complete to-dos in Google Tasks;
  • read email in Gmail and save drafts.

It never sends email and never deletes events, to-dos or email.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner their day and to answer their requests. It is stored on the owner's own server. Parts of it may be passed to the AI model that writes Jarvis's replies, only to answer the owner's request. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Jarvis's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
